# Barrage and indirect-fire abilities (CoH3 ability data)

Generated by `node tools/coh3/barrages.js`; do not edit. Damage is CoH3 per shell; total = shells × damage per shell.
Game columns (data/balance/indirect.json): one use every N turns, shells = CoH3 shells × N × 10 s ÷ CoH3 recharge (rounded up, accuracy × carries the rest), shell damage unchanged, each shell lands on a random hex within the spread, range = metres ÷ 7 capped per class.
Turns: recharge ÷ 10 s rounded up; hexes: metres ÷ 7 (data/balance/scale.json). Off-map strikes: CoH3 keeps their shells in custom_property_container/offmap, which coh3-data does not publish.
