## 1. What changed

### 1.1 Turn flow and reactions (D2-7)
- `EndTurn` / `StartMatch` commands. Turn end runs per-unit hooks in reverse axis order; turn start runs
  strikes → status → capture → victory → income (`src/logic/commands/phases.js`).
- Retreating units walk on by themselves at their turn start and spend that turn.
- Counter fire: once per enemy turn, armed at the enemy's turn start; needs the attacker in sight, range and **arc**.
  No counter from retreating, reloading or setting-up / packing-up units; overwatch and counter fire draw none.
- MG overwatch (`Hold`): emplaced HMG teams only; one burst at the first enemy stepping into arc, range and sight;
  the move stops if the mover dies or is pinned; such a move cannot be undone.
- Engaged mark (set by any attack), turret swings back at turn end when not engaged, crew shock lasts one turn.
- Reload timers: a weapon firing every N turns cannot fire or counter fire for N − 1 own turns.

### 1.2 Equipment (D2-7 + decisions)
- `Recrew` (towed guns, own or enemy), `Abandon` (crew steps beside the gun as its own unit), `ClearWreck` (engineers;
  vehicle wreck 2 turns, emplacement 1).
- Vehicles are never abandoned or captured; crewless guns are ownerless targets any side may destroy (CoH3).
- A crew without its gun fights with small arms.

### 1.3 Points and economy (D2-8)
- Capture: 1 point per capturing squad per own turn-start tick, 2 points per step, one step per tick; armed enemies
  contest; leaving loses progress. Sector supply links to a standing HQ (maps without sectors: every point pays).
- Income: HQ 60 MP / 5 fuel / 10 munitions (none while the HQ is destroyed); manpower points 70, fuel 12 + 10 MP,
  munitions 11 + 10 MP; a map point may set its own `yield`.
- Upkeep: 2 % of price, max 20 MP per unit, excess in fuel, net never below 25 % of gross. Population cap 300.
- `Produce`: beside a standing HQ or production building. Lists generated from CoH3 spawner data
  (`tools/coh3/production.js` → `data/rules/production.json`): units without a CoH3 building join the most similar one
  directly; only heroes, heavy armour and pricier variants need research; support vehicles in the Tier 2 building;
  conversion-only units are not produced.

### 1.4 Victory (D2-9) and buildings
- Capture mode: Victory Meter (VP-lead bleed 2 / 5 / 10, momentum, parity tiebreak) — the only win condition.
- Annihilation: destroy the enemy HQ or every enemy unit; optional round cap by surviving value.
- Every building can be destroyed in every mode: HQ 3000 HP, production buildings 1500 / 2000 (CoH3).
- A destroyed HQ can be rebuilt by engineers on its own footprint (500 MP, 2 turns, 50 % HP): one HQ per side.
- `Convert`: 23 CoH3 field conversions (`data/rules/conversions.json`); battlegroup-tied / global ones need research.
- D2 exit gate: `tests/integration/match.test.js` plays an annihilation and a capture match to the end via commands only.

## 3. Waiting for your decision

1. **Counter fire by suppressed / pinned units** — currently allowed (at ×0.5 / ×0.25 accuracy); v214 forbade both.
2. **Overwatch stop rule** — only a dead or pinned mover stops; a merely suppressed one keeps walking.
3. **Retreat arrival** — a unit reaching its rally point at its turn start cannot act that turn.
4. **Recrew squad size** — CoH3 max 5, so full 6-man rifle / grenadier squads cannot recrew a 57 mm / Pak 40.
5. **Capture speed** — at most one step per tick: two squads take a neutral point in one turn, an enemy one in two.
6. **Map building mapping** — German `support_center` → Support Armory, `motor_pool` → Mechanized Kompanie; British
   factions have no `support_center`; v214 doctrine buildings produce nothing.
7. **Research list contents** (11 units) and similarity placements (e.g. M29 Weasel in the US tank depot) — adjust with
   `place` / `research` in `data/rules/production.json`.
8. **Crusader III conversion cost** — your list 60 munitions; CoH3 data 45.
9. **HQ rebuild numbers** — 500 MP (CoH3 HQ cost), 2 turns, 50 % HP, engineers within 2 hexes (design values).
10. **Building HP** — CoH3 3000 / 1500 / 2000 replaced v214's HQ 690; houses stay 600 / 1200 / 2000, church 3000
    (design values). Armour 40 for HQ and production buildings is a design value.
11. **Earlier, still open** — sandbags / wire / tank traps show an HP bar; fire does not damage wrecks;
    suppression thresholds and critical chances (`data/balance/status.json`, `combat.json`) are design values.

## 4. Should be revised

| Item | Problem | Suggested fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Produced units face east | red units deploy facing away from the fight | face the enemy deploy zone or the map centre |
| Victory checked only at turn start | the win after a killing blow shows one turn late | also settle annihilation after `Attack` |
| Annihilation with an HQ rebuild | a destroyed HQ ends annihilation at the next turn start, so rebuilding never matters there | confirm, or give annihilation a grace turn |
| HMG / mortar team death leaves an "emplacement" wreck | a carried MG leaves the same wreck as a gun pit | no wreck for carried team weapons |
| Abandoning crew gets a fresh ammo load | free ammo by abandoning and recrewing | carry the gun's ammo over |
| `Recrewed` vs `Captured` | the previous owner of a crewless gun is not stored | store the last owner on decrew |
